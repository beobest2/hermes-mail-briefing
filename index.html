Hermes Mail Briefing

Hermes Mail Briefing is a private, single-user tool that reads the owner's own Gmail inboxes (read-only) and produces a short daily briefing for the owner.

  • Scope used: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly only.
  • It never sends, modifies, labels, or deletes mail.
  • It is not offered to the public; only the owner's own accounts are connected.
